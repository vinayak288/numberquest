    How to Play NumberQuest

    • Pick a difficulty: Easy (1–50), Normal (1–100), or Hard (1–500).
    • Type a number and press Enter or click Guess.
    • Watch the Possible Range bar shrink as you rule out numbers that are too high or too low.
    • Try to beat your personal best in the fewest attempts!
